# pi: privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. This policy covers the pi app for iPhone and iPad, made by Reindeer AI.

## What we collect

Nothing. pi has no account with us, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Reindeer AI doesn't receive your
sessions, files, messages, settings, or any information about how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

Your sessions, their files, the extensions you install, and your settings are stored on your device. If you turn on
iCloud Keychain sync in pi, your sign-ins, settings, extensions' settings, and vault items are kept in your iCloud
Keychain, end-to-end encrypted by Apple, so that they appear on your other devices.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

You choose where pi sends things; none of it goes to Reindeer AI.

- **AI models.** A session sends your messages, and the files and results the agent works with, to the AI model
  provider or server you set up (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or a server of your own). Their privacy
  policies apply to what they receive. A model downloaded to your device runs on the device and sends nothing.
- **The web.** The agent's browser visits the websites you or the agent open.
- **GitHub.** If you sign in with GitHub, pi uses your account for the repositories you ask the agent to work with.
- **Extensions.** Extensions you install can send data where their code says. You can read and change every
  extension's code in pi's Settings.

## Camera, microphone, and location

pi uses the microphone when you dictate a message, turning speech into text on your device when it can. An extension
can use the camera, the microphone, or your location only after you allow that extension; you can change that in
Settings → Extensions.

## The vault

Card and document numbers you keep in pi's vault are stored in your device's Keychain, behind Face ID. They're given to
an extension or a web page only after you approve each use.

## Children

pi is not directed at children.

## Changes and contact

We'll publish changes to this policy here, with a new effective date. pi's source code is public in this repository.
Questions: privacy@reindeer.ai.
